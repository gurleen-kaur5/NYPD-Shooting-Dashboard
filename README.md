# 🔫 NYC Shootings Dashboard  
📍 *Exploring Urban Violence Through Data*  
🚀 *Built entirely in Excel using slicers, charts, and story-driven visuals!*

---

## 📊 Project Overview  

After days of cleaning, designing, and analyzing — I'm thrilled to present my latest dashboard project focused on **NYC shooting incidents**.  
This Excel-based dashboard uncovers trends, highlights hotspots, and tells a story behind the data.

---

## 📌 Dashboard Highlights  

- **🔢 Total Incidents:** 28,562  
- **💀 Fatality Rate:** 19.3%  
- **🕚 Peak Hour:** 11 PM  
- **🏢 Top Precinct:** 75th Precinct  

---

## 🔥 Key Insights from Visuals  

### 1️⃣ When Do Most Shootings Occur?
- **🌙 Nighttime Dominance:** 56.6% of shootings occur between 8 PM–2 AM  
- **💥 Interpretation:** Crime surges when the city sleeps

### 2️⃣ Victim vs Perpetrator (Race-wise)
- **👥 Majority Involved:** Black individuals on both sides of the gun  
- **🔄 Insight:** A tragic cycle of internal community violence

### 3️⃣ Borough-wise Breakdown
- **🥧 Hotspots:** Brooklyn and the Bronx lead the count  
- **📍 Takeaway:** These areas require urgent socio-political attention

### 4️⃣ Jurisdiction Trends
- **📊 Transit-Linked Violence:** Code 1 (Transit) shows spikes at night  
- **🚇 Risk:** Public transport becomes more vulnerable after dark

### 5️⃣ Hotspot Precincts
- **📋 Most Affected:** 75th, 73rd, 40th precincts  
- **🧭 Observation:** A small number of precincts bear most of the burden

### 6️⃣ Monthly Patterns
- **🔻 Peak Months:** July & August see the highest surge  
- **🌡️ Possible Cause:** Summer heat may be linked to rising violence
